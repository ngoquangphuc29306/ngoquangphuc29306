## 👋 About Me

I'm a **third-year Software Engineering student** at **The University of Danang – University of Science and Technology (DUT)**, passionate about **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Software Engineering**.

What fascinates me most about AI is the mathematics and algorithms behind it. I've always been curious about how complex problems can be broken down into logical steps, how machines learn from data, and how mathematical concepts can be transformed into intelligent systems. For me, understanding **why something works** is just as important as knowing **how to make it work**.

My approach to learning combines **theory, hands-on experimentation, and research**. I enjoy exploring fundamental concepts, implementing algorithms, experimenting with different approaches, and understanding the reasoning behind their results. I believe that a strong theoretical foundation, combined with practical experience, is essential for becoming a capable engineer.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering**. Rather than focusing solely on training models, I want to understand the entire lifecycle of an AI-powered application — from data preparation and model development to backend integration, deployment, and continuous improvement.

My long-term goal is to become an **AI/ML Engineer** capable of designing and building **end-to-end AI systems** that are not only intelligent but also reliable, scalable, and genuinely useful in real-world applications.

I know there's still a long way to go, and that's what makes this journey exciting. Every new concept, challenging problem, and project is an opportunity to improve my understanding and become a better engineer.

### 🎯 What I'm Focusing On

- 🧠 **Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning:** Exploring neural networks, machine learning algorithms, PyTorch, and the mathematical foundations of modern AI.
- 🤖 **LLMs & Intelligent Systems:** Learning about Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI agents, and how to integrate language models into practical applications.
- ⚙️ **Software Engineering & System Design:** Building a stronger understanding of backend development, software architecture, clean code, and maintainable systems.
- 🔬 **Research & Problem Solving:** Developing analytical thinking through mathematical reasoning, algorithmic problem-solving, experiments, and research.
- 🚀 **End-to-End AI Development:** Working toward the ability to design, develop, deploy, and improve complete AI-powered applications.

### 🌍 Beyond the Code

Life isn't all about writing code, training models, or solving complex problems. I believe there's much more to explore and experience beyond the screen.

- 🏋️ **Fitness & Gym:** I enjoy working out, challenging my limits, and staying physically and mentally strong.
- ✈️ **Travel & Exploration:** I love traveling, discovering new places, experiencing different cultures, and creating unforgettable memories along the way.
- 🌅 **Enjoying Life:** Whether it's spending time with friends, exploring somewhere new, or simply appreciating the little moments, I believe in making the most of life.
- 🌱 **Personal Growth:** I'm always looking for ways to improve myself, embrace new experiences, and maintain a healthy balance between ambition and happiness.

At the end of the day, I want to build a meaningful career, explore the world, and enjoy the journey along the way.

> 💭 *"Work hard, stay curious, explore the world, and don't forget to enjoy the little things."*
